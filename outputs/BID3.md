# BID3

Riverton Unified School District is seeking bids for three specific educational technology products: the Lumetra LX-770i interactive laser projector, Lumetra WM-200 ultra short throw wall mount, and Lumetra DC-40 document camera under bid number RUSD-2025-014. Bids must be submitted by March 21, 2025, at 3:00 PM PST via bid bond, certified check, or cashier's check. The contract term is two years with renewal options, delivery occurs within 60 calendar days after purchase order, and bids must be submitted through the Valley Schools Purchasing Cooperative. A pre-bid meeting is scheduled for February 25, 2025, at 10:00 AM PST in Room (non-mandatory), and a 5% bid bond of the total proposal amount is required.
